Privacy Policy

Binary Practice · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Binary Practice helps you practise converting numbers between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal. There are no accounts and no servers of our own. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect some information from your device as described below.

Stored on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it can work the way you left it: your settings (such as keypad, sound and vibration), your best score in each single player mode, and counters that limit how often ads appear.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. If your phone backs up app data, your phone's backup service may keep a copy under its own terms.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address or location, and the app has no sign-in.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads, native ads, full-screen ads between games, an ad when the app opens, and optional videos you can choose to watch to continue a game. To show, measure and protect ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised based on your activity, where the law and your choices allow it.

We may also use other ad networks through AdMob mediation. They handle information in the same way for their own ads and have their own privacy policies.

To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before it shows ads. Where this applies, you can review or change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in Settings & Info. This entry appears only where a choice is required.

On your phone you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in the device's Google settings (on Android: Settings, Google, Ads). You can still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Your rights

We do not hold personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Ads are provided by Google and its partners, who handle the information they collect under their own policies; you can manage your ad settings as described in Your choices.

Children

Binary Practice is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and its "last updated" date.
